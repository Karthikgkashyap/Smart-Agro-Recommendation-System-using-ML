PARAMETER GUIDE

Understanding Your Inputs

Nitrogen (N)

Essential for leaf growth and chlorophyll. High N favours leafy crops like rice and jute.

Phosphorus (P)

Promotes root development and flowering. Critical for legumes and fruit crops.

Potassium (K)

Regulates water uptake and disease resistance. High K benefits cotton and bananas.

Temperature

The most climate-sensitive parameter. Tropical crops need >25°C; temperate crops prefer 10–20°C.

Humidity

High humidity suits rice, jute, and coconut. Low humidity favours chickpea and lentils.

Soil pH

Most crops prefer 6–7. Blueberries like acidic (4.5); some crops tolerate alkaline (>7.5).

Rainfall

The #1 predictor in this model. Rice needs 200mm+; mungbean thrives under 40mm.
